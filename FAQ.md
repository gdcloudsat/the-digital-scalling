# FAQ

## General
### What is The Digital Scaling?
It's a specialized CMS for digital agencies to manage their portfolio, services, and blog while capturing leads.

### Is it free?
Yes, it's released under the MIT License.

## Technical
### Which PHP version is required?
PHP 8.2 or higher is required.

### Can I use PostgreSQL?
Yes, it's compatible with any database supported by Laravel (MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite, SQL Server).

### How do I change the primary color?
You can change the primary color in the Admin Panel under **Settings -> General Settings**.

## Usage
### How do I add a new project?
Go to **Projects** in the admin sidebar and click **New Project**. Fill in the details and upload your media.

### Can I schedule blog posts?
Yes, set the `Published At` date to a future time, and the post will only appear on the site once that time has passed.
