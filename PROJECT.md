# The Digital Scaling - Digital Agency Portfolio CMS

The Digital Scaling is a high-performance, modern Digital Agency Portfolio CMS built with Laravel, Filament, and Tailwind CSS. It's designed to provide digital agencies with a professional platform to showcase their services, projects, and insights while managing leads and content efficiently.

## Project Overview

- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **License:** MIT
- **Tech Stack:** Laravel 11, Filament v3, Tailwind CSS, Spatie Media Library, GSAP, AOS.
- **Repository:** [https://github.com/gdcloudsat/the-digital-scalling](https://github.com/gdcloudsat/the-digital-scalling)

## Mission

To provide a seamless, content-rich experience for digital agencies to present their brand and attract high-quality leads through a modern, responsive, and easy-to-manage web platform.

## Core Values

- **Performance:** Optimized for speed and Core Web Vitals.
- **Scalability:** Built on Laravel's robust foundation.
- **User Experience:** Smooth animations and intuitive navigation.
- **Manageability:** Powerful admin panel powered by Filament.
